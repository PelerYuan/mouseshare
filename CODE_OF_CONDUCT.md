# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our pledge

We want mouseshare to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful and patient, especially with newcomers;
- giving and graciously accepting specific, constructive feedback;
- disagreeing about code and ideas, never about people;
- assuming good faith and remembering that maintainers and contributors are
  volunteers with limited time;
- writing in a way that non-native English speakers can follow, and welcoming
  contributions in any language we can translate.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, trolling;
- discriminatory language or imagery;
- publishing others' private information without permission;
- sustained disruption of discussions, or pressuring maintainers for
  responses or merges;
- anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, and any place where someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, lock or delete comments, issues and pull
requests that violate this code, and may warn, temporarily block, or
permanently ban contributors for behaviour they consider inappropriate.

To report a concern privately, use GitHub's
**[private vulnerability / conduct report](https://github.com/PelerYuan/mouseshare/security/advisories/new)**
form and mark it as a Code of Conduct report, or contact the repository owner
through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially, and
maintainers who are involved in an incident will not take part in deciding its
outcome.
