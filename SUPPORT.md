# Getting help

[简体中文](SUPPORT.zh-CN.md)

- **How do I…?** Read the [User Guide](docs/USER_GUIDE.md) and the
  [Troubleshooting & FAQ](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
- **Questions and ideas:** open a thread in
  [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/PelerYuan/mouseshare/discussions).
- **Something is broken:** open a
  [bug report](https://github.com/PelerYuan/mouseshare/issues/new/choose) with the
  details listed in the template.
- **A security problem:** do **not** open a public issue — see
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

mouseshare is maintained by volunteers; replies may take a few days.
